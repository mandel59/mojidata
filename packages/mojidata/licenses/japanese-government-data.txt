Mojidata imports the extracted JSON data from these pinned npm packages:
- @mandel59/joyokanjihyo 1.2.0 (joyokanjihyo.json)
- @mandel59/nyukanseiji 1.2.0 (nyukanseiji.json)

The packages' source declarations are preserved in sources/joyo-README.md and
sources/nyukan-README.md, including their original article-reference typo.
Their MIT-0 statements concern the extraction programs. Mojidata imports the
government-notice data, not those extraction programs.

Affected data: joyo, joyo_acceptable, joyo_kangxi; nyukan_itaiji, nyukan_ruiji,
and the nyukan view. The JSON is normalized into SQLite tables.
